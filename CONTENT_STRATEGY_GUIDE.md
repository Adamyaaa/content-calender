# 📅 Company Content Strategy & Publishing Guide

A complete, battle-tested framework for planning, producing, approving, and distributing high-impact content across all company channels.

---

## 1. The Core Content Pillars (The 40-30-20-10 Rule)

To keep content balanced and prevent your feeds from becoming purely promotional, balance posts across four pillars:

| Pillar | Target % | Purpose | Example Formats |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Educational / Value-First** | **40%** | Solve customer pain points, teach frameworks, build authority. | How-to carousels, cheat sheets, teardowns, industry benchmarks. |
| **2. Thought Leadership & Perspective**| **30%** | Share contrarian views, industry trends, lessons from building. | Founder insights, predictions, opinion threads, key takeaways. |
| **3. Social Proof & Case Studies** | **20%** | Prove ROI, demonstrate customer wins, build trust and credibility. | Customer spotlights, transformation numbers, before/after stories. |
| **4. Product Announcements & Culture**| **10%** | Show feature launches, team milestones, behind-the-scenes culture. | Changelog updates, new feature videos, hiring & office moments. |

## 3. Production & Approval Workflow (Stages)

1. **💡 Idea / Brainstorm**: Raw concepts, trending topics, customer questions. Backlog stage.
2. **✍️ Drafting**: Content creator drafts copy, defines media specs, and creates hooks.
3. **🔍 In Review**: Peer or lead review for tone, brand guidelines, factual accuracy, and design assets.
4. **🚀 Scheduled / Ready**: Final assets attached, copy proofread, scheduled in distribution tool.
5. **📢 Published & Engaging**: Post is live. The first 60 minutes are critical: reply to all early comments to boost algorithmic distribution.
6. **📊 Analyzed**: Log impressions, click-through rates, and lead conversions to double-down on winners.

## 5. Proven Copywriting Frameworks

- **PAS (Problem - Agitate - Solve):**
  - *Problem*: "Most B2B content gets zero engagement."
  - *Agitate*: "You spend 4 hours writing a post, only for 12 people to see it and 0 leads to book."
  - *Solve*: "Here is the exact 3-step hook formula that took our impressions from 10k to 250k."
- **AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action):**
  - *Attention*: Bold first line or counter-intuitive stat.
  - *Interest*: Back it up with context or data.
  - *Desire*: Show how it makes their work 10x easier.
  - *Action*: Clear Call To Action (Save, Share, Link in bio, Comment).
- **BAB (Before - After - Bridge):**
  - *Before*: Where the customer was stuck.
  - *After*: What their business looks like today.
  - *Bridge*: How your product/method made it happen.
